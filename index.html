About & How to Use

Civil Engineering Reference for Licensure Examination Digital Answer Key System is a practice exam tool designed to test and grade your answers against the reference book reviewers.

🚀 Quick Start Guide
  1. Select Reference & Session: Choose the Reference Volume and Exam Session from the top dropdowns.
  2. Choose Subject: Switch tabs between MSTEC, HPGE, and PSAD.
  3. Start Exam: Press Start in the timer box to unlock answer choices and begin timing.
  4. Answer & Flag: Select choices (A–E) for each item. Use 🏳️ to flag questions for review.
  5. Submit: Click Submit Exam to view your score, breakdown, and itemized answer review.
⏱️ Standard Board Sequence and Timers
  • PSAD (Principles of Structural Analysis & Design): 6 Hours
  • MSTEC (Mathematics, Surveying, Transportation Engineering, and Construction): 5 Hours
  • HPGE (Hydraulics & Principles of Geotechnical Engineering): 4 Hours
💾 Saved Progress & Attempt History

Your exam attempt history is saved directly on your browser device. Refreshing the page resets active unsubmitted exams to ensure fresh practice attempts.
